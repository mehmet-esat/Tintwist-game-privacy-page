Tintwist Privacy Policy

Lunavex Games · Effective October 29, 2026

Tintwist is a color sorting puzzle game. This policy explains what the game does with information on your device. In short: the game does not collect, share or sell personal information.

Information we collect

None. Tintwist has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics. It does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information, and it does not send your game data to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small amount of data in your device's local storage:

  • the levels you have unlocked and your best star rating per level,
  • your coin balance and the themes and tube styles you own,
  • your settings (sound, vibration, theme).

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Device features

The game can use your device's vibration for short taps of feedback. You can turn this off in Settings. The game needs no other permissions.

Advertising

The current version shows no ads. If a future version adds optional ads (for example, watching an ad for extra coins), this policy will be updated before that version is released to describe the ad provider and the data it uses.

Children

Tintwist does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
