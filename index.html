Tintwist Privacy Policy

Lunavex Games · Effective October 31, 2026

Tintwist is a color sorting puzzle game. This policy explains what information the game uses and who receives it. In short: we, Lunavex Games, do not collect, share or sell personal information. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google uses some device information to show and measure them.

Information we collect

None. Tintwist has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics of its own. It does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information, and it does not send your game data to us.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small amount of data in your device's local storage:

  • the levels you have unlocked and your best star rating per level,
  • your coin balance and the themes and ball styles you own,
  • your daily reward and daily challenge progress,
  • your settings (sound, vibration, language, theme).

Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this data in your device backup so your progress can come back on a new phone; that backup is handled by Google under your account settings, and we cannot see it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between levels from time to time, and optional ads you choose to watch for a reward (double coins or an extra tube). To show, limit and measure these ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with the ads. Google may use it for personalized ads where the law and your choices allow it.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings (Google > Ads).
  • How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Device features

The game can use your device's vibration for short taps of feedback. You can turn this off in Settings. The app uses the internet only to load ads.

Children

Tintwist is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
